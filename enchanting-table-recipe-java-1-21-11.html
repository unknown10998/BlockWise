How to make a enchanting table in Minecraft

Place a book in the top-middle slot, diamonds on the left and right of the middle row, one obsidian in the center, and three obsidian across the bottom.

Enchanting Table recipe ingredients

You need: 1 book, 2 diamonds, and 4 obsidian. The recipe makes 1 Enchanting Table.

This layout is shaped: the book, diamonds, and obsidian must occupy the indicated slots.

What is a enchanting table used for?

Use an enchanting table with lapis lazuli and experience. Bookshelves placed correctly around the table unlock higher enchantment offers.
