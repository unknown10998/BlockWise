Public searches performed for `BlockWise Minecraft tools commands recipes`, `"unknown10998.github.io/BlockWise"`, and `site:unknown10998.github.io/BlockWise/` did not surface a clearly established result for this BlockWise project in the retrieved samples. This is **not** proof Google has not indexed it: query results vary by time, region and engine, and only a verified Search Console property reports indexing and impressions authoritatively.

`BlockWise` is not a unique name. Other projects and businesses use similar names, including a Minecraft build-guide site at https://blockwise.guide/ and non-Minecraft sites. Do not impersonate or use their trademarks/designs. Consistently use the longer brand identity **BlockWise — Minecraft Recipes, Commands & Calculators**, alongside the site URL.

## What to publish next

Consider 2–4 carefully verified, original guides per month for topics that Minecraft players actually search for, such as a specific Java 1.21.11 command, a crafting recipe with alternative materials, or a concrete Minecraft storage/building calculation. For each:
- match a real player question with a **specific searchable title** like `Minecraft Hopper Recipe (Java 1.21.11) — BlockWise`;
- put the answer, ingredients or command, version, caveats and examples in static HTML;
- link to an interactive BlockWise feature and back to related topics;
- cite a version-matched factual source when needed;
- preserve real dates, canonical URLs, sitemap entry and tests;
- update/remove incorrect guidance when game versions change.

Use the 6 new recipe pages as a **quality baseline**, not a template for hundreds of duplicate pages. The recipe browser still covers the full dataset interactively, and most of those results do not warrant a separate article without additional player value.

## Tracking success — avoid ranking promises

When Search Console becomes available, check **queries, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position and indexing** for the brand phrase `BlockWise Minecraft` versus specific queries like `minecraft chest recipe`, `minecraft knockback 255 stick command`, and `minecraft java 1.21.11 enchantment order optimizer`. These are topic candidates, **not** verified ranking or low-competition keyword claims. Do not assume you will rank above the official Minecraft Wiki or major established sites.

There is no free switch that guarantees the site will be first for `BlockWise` or for individual crafting/command queries. Search ranking comes from Google's discovery, relevance, quality, reputation and other systems; snippets and title links may be automatically rewritten. Google AI Overviews/AI Mode have no special submit-to-cite guarantee.

## Important limitation: GitHub Pages project-path site names

Google's **site name** feature is supported at a domain/subdomain level, *not* separately for the path of a GitHub Pages project (`unknown10998.github.io/BlockWise/`). This means the presence of `WebSite` structured data under the project path cannot guarantee Google will display **BlockWise** as the SERP site-name label, though page title links may still show `— BlockWise`. See https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names. A dedicated subdomain/hostname would provide better brand control, but is not required for search indexing and does **not** guarantee better rankings. Avoid purchasing a domain until there is a clear budget and need.
