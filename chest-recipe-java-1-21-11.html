Step-by-step crafting instructions

Surround the outside of a 3×3 crafting grid with eight wooden planks, leaving the center empty.

The recipe accepts the wooden plank variants in Java Edition 1.21.11. A chest holds 27 inventory slots; placing two compatible chests side by side makes a large chest.

What is a chest used for?

Build a chest for early-game storage, or use it as an ingredient for hoppers and other storage components.
