Privacy Policy

This policy explains the current data behavior of BlockWise and the changes that must happen before advertising is activated. It is an information page, not a substitute for legal advice.

Who this policy covers

BlockWise is an independent Minecraft fan website providing browser-based tools, guides, recipe exploration, command references, and farm references. The project does not currently publish a personal operator identity or public contact method. Before monetization or another activity that requires a public owner/contact path, the responsible adult owner must provide one without exposing a minor's personal information.

Read About BlockWise for the site's purpose, feature limits, and independent-fan-project statement.

Information BlockWise currently handles

BlockWise does not currently provide user accounts, a newsletter, a contact form, comments, payments, or a server-side database. The site does not include Google AdSense, Google Analytics, or another advertising/analytics tag in its current source.

Some tools save information only in the browser on the device being used:

  • Appearance settings such as theme, text size, spacing, contrast, and reduced-motion preference.
  • Smart Command Finder favorites and recent command configurations.

This data is stored with browser localStorage when it is available. It is not sent by BlockWise to a BlockWise server. You can remove it through your browser's site-data controls; clearing it resets the related preferences or saved command entries.

Cookies and similar technologies

BlockWise's current first-party code uses localStorage for the browser-local features described above. It does not currently set an advertising cookie or include an ad tag. Your browser, hosting provider, and any third-party content you choose to load can still use their own cookies, logs, or similar technologies under their own policies.

Third-party services and links

BlockWise is served through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as request, security, and operational log data when serving the site; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Some Farm Library cards embed YouTube using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, and cards can link to YouTube, Minecraft Wiki, Minecraft.net, and other external resources. Privacy-enhanced embedding does not mean YouTube collects no data. A third party's policies apply when its content or link is loaded, and BlockWise does not control those services.

Advertising status and future changes

Advertising is not active on BlockWise today. If the responsible owner later enables Google AdSense or another advertising service, this policy must be updated before activation to identify the advertising provider, cookies or local storage used, data-sharing disclosures, user choices, and any required vendor information.

Google states that AdSense publishers must provide a privacy policy about cookie use. For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, publishers serving relevant ads may need an appropriate Google-certified consent-management solution and region-specific disclosures. The owner must review current Google requirements and applicable law before adding any advertising code.

Children's privacy

BlockWise is designed to be useful to Minecraft players of varied ages, but it is not directed at collecting personal information from children. Because the site has no account or contact form, BlockWise does not currently ask visitors to submit personal information. A responsible adult should review children's-privacy, consent, and advertising obligations before adding accounts, forms, analytics, personalized advertising, or any feature that collects information.

Retention and deletion

Browser-local preferences and saved command entries remain on your device until you clear them or the browser removes them. BlockWise has no user account database to delete. Third-party services have their own retention and deletion processes; use their privacy controls or policies for requests relating to those services.

Policy updates

BlockWise may update this policy as features, hosting, advertising, or legal requirements change. The last-updated date at the top will change when material revisions are published. Material future changes—especially enabling advertising, analytics, or data-collection features—must be documented before or when they take effect as applicable.

Questions or concerns

A public contact method has not yet been provided by the responsible owner. Before monetization, business activity, or any situation where a public contact route is required, the responsible adult owner must publish an appropriate contact method and update this policy. Do not send personal information to unofficial third-party links claiming to represent BlockWise.
