Step-by-step crafting instructions

Place one coal or charcoal above one stick, in a vertical line, to craft four torches.

The two ingredients can be arranged in a crafting table or your inventory crafting grid. Coal and charcoal are alternatives; the remaining slots stay empty.

What is a torch used for?

Torches light up caves and bases, helping you navigate and reduce hostile mob spawning in dark areas.
