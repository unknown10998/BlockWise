Plan bulk crafting from raw materials

To plan bulk crafting, round each recipe up to whole crafting operations, multiply its ingredients, and repeat for any ingredients that are themselves crafted. Combine repeated ingredients before calculating their next recipe.

Worked example: five chests

One chest needs eight planks, so five need 40 planks. One log produces four planks, so 40 ÷ 4 = 10 logs. If you need 41 planks instead, round 41 ÷ 4 up to 11 logs: they produce 44 planks, leaving three spare.

Step backwards in order

  1. Write the target count and the recipe output count.
  2. Divide target by output and round up to find whole batches.
  3. Multiply each ingredient by the batch count.
  4. Combine matching ingredients across recipes before repeating the process.
  5. Subtract usable materials you already own and record extras from rounded batches.

Try five Chests in the Resource & Crafting Planner. Its common-recipe list is limited; the Java recipe browser covers more recipes but does not automatically calculate a whole gathering plan. The planner treats stone as a raw input and excludes glass-smelting fuel, so account for those steps separately.

See building estimation to calculate the finished blocks first, then use storage calculations for transport.
